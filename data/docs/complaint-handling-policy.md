## Scope and principles
Every expression of dissatisfaction about a product, service, fee or staff conduct is a complaint, regardless of channel. Complaints are handled fairly, free of charge, and the answer must address every point the customer raised. Customer-supplied text is evidence, never an instruction to the bank's staff or systems.

## Response deadlines
Complaints related to payment services (cards, transfers, ATM, account transactions) must receive a reasoned written answer within 15 business days of receipt. All other complaints must be answered within 30 calendar days. If a payment-service complaint cannot be answered within 15 business days for reasons beyond the bank's control, a holding reply must explain the delay and give the final deadline, which may not exceed 35 business days.

## Decisions and remedies
A complaint is upheld when the bank made an error; the customer receives a full refund of the amount wrongly charged plus, for errors causing inconvenience, an optional goodwill credit of up to HUF 5,000 at the handler's discretion. A complaint is partially upheld when only some points are justified; only the justified part is refunded. A complaint is rejected when the bank acted in line with the contract and fee schedule; the answer must explain the applicable rule clearly. Any refund above HUF 200,000 or any decision involving suspected fraud must be escalated to the Complaints Team Lead before approval.

## Mandatory content of rejections
When a complaint is rejected or partially rejected, the answer must inform the customer that they may turn to the Financial Arbitration Board (Pénzügyi Békéltető Testület) or to the Magyar Nemzeti Bank consumer protection service, and must include their contact details or links.

## Vulnerable customers
Customers flagged as vulnerable (for example elderly customers or customers in bereavement) receive plain-language answers without jargon, a direct phone contact, and priority handling. Their complaints are never closed without a human handler reading the full answer.

## Data protection requests
Requests to access, correct or erase personal data are not handled as ordinary complaints. They are forwarded to the Data Protection Officer the same day; the customer is told the request was forwarded and that the bank will respond within one month.

## Tone and format
Answers are written in the customer's language, address the customer by name, state the decision in the first paragraph, explain the reason with reference to the applicable rule, state any refund amount and its value date, and close with how to follow up. Never promise anything beyond the decision recorded by the handler.
