## Unauthorised transactions
If a customer reports a card or account transaction they did not authorise, the bank refunds the amount immediately and at the latest by the end of the following business day after the report. The only exception is when the bank has reasonable grounds to suspect fraud by the customer and reports this in writing to the supervisory authority. The refund may not be delayed while the bank investigates the customer's possible negligence; liability is assessed afterwards.

## Customer liability
Before the loss or theft of the card is reported, the customer may bear losses up to HUF 15,000, unless the transaction was made without strong customer authentication, in which case the customer bears no loss. Online transactions without strong customer authentication (SCA) are refunded in full.

## Card blocking
When an unauthorised transaction is reported, the card is blocked immediately and a replacement card is sent free of charge.

## Merchant disputes (chargeback)
If goods or services paid by card were not delivered or were materially different from what was ordered, the customer should first contact the merchant. If the merchant does not resolve the issue, the bank initiates a chargeback through the card scheme. The chargeback is not a refund decision of the bank: the outcome depends on the merchant's response, typically within 45 days. The answer to the customer must explain that the chargeback was initiated, what documents are needed (order confirmation, correspondence with the merchant), and the expected timeline.

## Duplicate settlement
If the same card authorisation (same authorisation code) was settled twice, the second debit is an error of the bank and is refunded in full without waiting for the merchant.
