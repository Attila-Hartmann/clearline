## Loan installment allocation
Loan installments are due on the contractual due day. A payment received on or before the due day is on time. Reminder letters are generated three business days after the due day using the loan balance at the time of generation; a reminder generated from an outdated balance snapshot is an operational error and its fee is refunded.

## Payment holiday and hardship
Customers facing temporary financial hardship may request a payment rescheduling. Such requests are routed to the Collections Hardship Team and are not decided in the complaint answer; the answer confirms the routing and the expected contact within 5 business days.

## Account closure
The customer may terminate the account agreement at any time with immediate effect. Closure is free after 6 months; earlier closure carries the fee in the fee schedule. Remaining balance is transferred to the account specified by the customer.

## Statements and communication preferences
Customers can switch between paper and electronic statements in the mobile app or at a branch. The change takes effect from the next statement cycle.

## Branch service standards
Staff must treat customers courteously, explain products in plain language and offer seating and priority service to elderly or disabled customers. Complaints about staff conduct are investigated by the branch manager, who may contact the customer; the answer apologises where the standard was not met and explains the follow-up action.
