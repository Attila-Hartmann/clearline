## Account packages
The Basic account costs HUF 990 per month. The Plus package costs HUF 2,490 per month. The Plus monthly fee is waived for a calendar month if incoming transfers credited in that month total at least HUF 250,000. The waiver is applied automatically at month end; if it is not applied although the condition was met, the fee must be refunded in full.

## Debit cards
The debit card annual fee is HUF 3,900. Cards on the Plus package are free of annual fee.

## Cash withdrawals
At Danubia ATMs the first two cash withdrawals per calendar month are free. From the third withdrawal in the same month the fee is 0.6% of the amount, minimum HUF 200. At other banks' ATMs the fee is 1% of the amount plus HUF 400. If an ATM debits the account but does not dispense cash, the debited amount and any related fee are refunded after the ATM journal is checked.

## Foreign currency card payments
Card payments in a currency other than HUF are converted at the Danubia card exchange rate and carry a fee of 1.5% of the HUF amount. The fee is shown as a separate line on the statement.

## Statements
Electronic statements are free. A monthly paper statement costs HUF 500. Customers who switched to e-statements must not be charged the paper statement fee from the month following the switch.

## Loan payment reminders
A payment reminder letter costs HUF 2,500. It may be charged only if the installment is unpaid on the date the reminder is generated, and at most once per loan per calendar month. Reminder fees charged in breach of these conditions are refunded.

## Account closure
Closing an account is free if the account has been open for at least 6 months. If the account is closed within 6 months of opening, a closure fee of HUF 2,000 applies.
